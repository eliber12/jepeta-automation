    A FEW IMPORTANT DETAILS

    Know what
    you’re looking at.

    Does a low score mean a token is safe?+

    No. The score summarizes observed risk signals; it is not a probability, an audit, or a guarantee. Contracts, liquidity, and data can change. Your execution decision remains yours.

    Which tokens can I scan?+

    ERC-20 token contracts on Base, chain ID 8453. Use the exact contract address, not a wallet address or ticker. A result depends on source coverage for that token.

    What happens when data is missing?+

    If required security data is unavailable, the scanner returns an error rather than a reassuring verdict. Other limitations can appear as warnings or a reduced data-quality assessment.

    Do I need to connect my wallet?+

    No wallet connection or payment is needed for the free preview. Paid reports are purchased separately through Virtuals ACP. Jepeta does not execute trades.
